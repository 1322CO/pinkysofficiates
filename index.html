Meet Chelsie

If you're looking for someone who will laugh with you, cry with you, calm your nerves before you walk down the aisle, and tell your love story in a way that feels genuine‚Äîyou've found your person.

With more than 20 years in the event industry, I know that the most meaningful moments aren't always the perfectly planned ones. They're the unexpected glances, shaky vows, happy tears, laughter, and the feeling that everyone in the room is witnessing something truly special.

My journey as an officiant began in 2019, when two close friends asked me to marry them during an intimate elopement in Kauai, Hawaii. Standing beneath a waterfall, surrounded by nature and the people they loved, I felt something I couldn't quite explain. I simply knew I was exactly where I was meant to be.

I thought it would be my first and last ceremony.

Instead, it became the beginning of something I never saw coming.

Since then, one ceremony has led to another through referrals, friends, and couples who connected with the way I tell a love story. And every ceremony reminds me why I said yes that first time.

I don't believe your wedding should sound like it came from a template. It should sound like you.
